Frequently Asked Questions

Are there long-term contracts?

No — all our retainers are month-to-month. You can adjust, upgrade, or pause your service anytime with 30 days notice.

Can I customize a package to suit specific needs?

Yes! We often build custom hybrid retainers (e.g. SEO + Paid Ads + Landing Page Design) tailored to your exact budget and timeline.

Is ad spend included in the monthly retainer fee?

No, ad spend is paid directly to advertising platforms (Google, Meta, LinkedIn). Our retainer covers campaign strategy, design, copywriting, management, and optimization.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfers, UPI, credit cards, and auto-debit invoices for smooth monthly billing.
