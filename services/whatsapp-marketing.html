Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions About WhatsApp Marketing

What is the difference between WhatsApp Business App and WhatsApp Business API?

The free WhatsApp Business App is designed for small solo businesses with a 256-contact broadcast limit and single-device limitations. The official WhatsApp Business API (Cloud API) allows unlimited multi-agent team inboxes, automated AI chatbots, CRM sync, green tick verification, and sending thousands of approved bulk broadcasts without phone number bans.

Will our phone number get banned for sending bulk marketing messages?

Using unauthorized third-party scraping tools or personal numbers guarantees permanent bans. Growell Marketing provisions the Official Meta Cloud API where all broadcast templates are pre-approved by Meta and opt-in guidelines are strictly followed, maintaining a high messaging quality tier and 100% account safety.

How does WhatsApp automation recover abandoned e-commerce carts?

When a customer adds items to their Shopify or WooCommerce cart but exits before purchasing, our webhook triggers an automated WhatsApp message within 15 to 30 minutes. It displays product images, customer name, and a one-click checkout button with an optional discount, routinely recovering 20% to 35% of lost revenue.

How do Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) Ads work?

Click-to-WhatsApp ads run across Facebook and Instagram feeds. When a user taps your ad, it opens a direct WhatsApp chat window rather than a slow landing page. Our automated chatbot immediately greets them, qualifies their budget or inquiry, and routes high-intent leads to your sales reps in real time.

Can we integrate WhatsApp with our existing CRM or Shopify store?

Yes. We integrate WhatsApp with Shopify, WooCommerce, Zoho, HubSpot, Leadsquared, Google Sheets, and custom webhooks. All customer actions (orders, inquiries, sign-ups) trigger automated real-time messages instantly.
